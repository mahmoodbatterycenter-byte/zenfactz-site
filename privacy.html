Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers the ZenFactz Uploader, a private desktop tool used by the owner of the ZenFactz YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

  • Your Google account's YouTube channel (name, channel ID, video list).
  • The ability to upload videos and edit metadata on that same channel.

The tool requests the youtube scope and acts only on the channel that authorised it.

What is stored, and where

  • An OAuth refresh token, stored locally on the owner's computer in token.json. It never leaves that machine.
  • A local log of uploads (file name, video ID, privacy status, time).

No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs.

What is never done

  • No data is sold, shared, rented or transferred to third parties.
  • No advertising or profiling. No data is used to train models.
  • No data belonging to any other channel or user is accessed.

Deleting your data

Revoke access at any time from Google Account › Security › Third-party access. Deleting token.json and state/ledger.json on the local machine removes the stored token and upload history.
